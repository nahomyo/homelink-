1. Product Overview

Product Name

HomeLink

Product Vision

HomeLink is a trusted digital platform that connects domestic workers, households, and brokers (delalas) in one secure marketplace, making domestic employment easier, safer, and more transparent.

Mission

To modernize domestic employment through technology by providing a trusted platform where workers can find dignified jobs, households can hire confidently, and brokers can operate transparently.


---

2. Problem Statement

Domestic employment is largely informal.

Current challenges include:

Difficult to find trustworthy workers

Workers struggle to find jobs

Heavy dependence on brokers

Lack of verification

No transparent hiring process

No employment history

Poor communication

High recruitment costs

Limited accountability

Goals:

Manage workers

Manage employers

Track placements

Earn commissions

Commission policy:

HomeLink receives a transparent commission from the household/employer only after a successful hire is completed. Workers do not pay registration, application, placement, or commission fees. The commission amount and payment timing must be shown to the household before hiring is confirmed.

19. MVP Definition of Success

The first version of HomeLink will be considered successful if it can:

Enable verified registration for workers, households, and brokers.

Allow households to post jobs and workers to apply.

Support secure messaging between users.

Reduce the average hiring process compared to informal methods.

Build trust through verification and two-way ratings.

Demonstrate strong user engagement and repeat usage in a pilot city.
